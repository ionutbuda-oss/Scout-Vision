# ScoutVision Competency Framework v1.0

The framework is built from measurable Wyscout KPIs.
Every competency must be explainable by underlying data.

---

## DISTRIBUTION

Purpose:
Ability to circulate possession accurately.

KPIs:

- Passes per 90
- Accurate passes %
- Long passes per 90
- Accurate long passes %

---

## PROGRESSION

Purpose:
Ability to advance possession.

KPIs:

- Progressive passes per 90
- Accurate progressive passes %
- Forward passes per 90
- Accurate forward passes %
- Passes to final third per 90
- Accurate passes to final third %
- Through passes per 90
- Accurate through passes %

---

## CREATIVITY

Purpose:
Ability to create scoring opportunities.

KPIs:

- Smart passes per 90
- Accurate smart passes %
- Assists per 90
- xA per 90

---

## BALL CARRYING

Purpose:
Ability to progress with the ball.

KPIs:

- Dribbles per 90
- Successful dribbles %
- Successful attacking actions per 90

---

## DEFENDING

Purpose:
Ability to prevent opposition progression.

KPIs:

- Successful defensive actions per 90
- Defensive duels per 90
- Defensive duels won %
- Interceptions per 90

---

## OFFENSIVE DUELS

Purpose:
Ability to win individual attacking situations.

KPIs:

- Offensive duels per 90
- Offensive duels won %

---

## GOAL THREAT

Purpose:
Ability to generate and convert scoring chances.

KPIs:

- Goals per 90
- xG per 90
- Goal conversion %
- Touches in box per 90

---

## CHANCE CREATION

Purpose:
Ability to create opportunities from wide areas.

KPIs:

- Crosses per 90
- Accurate crosses %
- xA per 90
