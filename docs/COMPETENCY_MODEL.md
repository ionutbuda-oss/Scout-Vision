# ScoutVision Competency Model v1.0

## Rule 1
Every DNA profile must be supported by measurable competencies.
No profile may rely on unavailable data.

---

## Centre Back (CB)

Available competencies:

- Defending
- Ball Winning
- Distribution
- Progression

Forbidden profiles:

- Aerial Defender (no aerial metrics)
- No-Nonsense Defender (requires clearances/blocks)
- Sweeper (requires recovery actions)

Candidate profiles:

- Ball Playing Defender
- Front Foot Defender
- Defensive Stopper
- Cover Defender
- Defensive Organizer

---
