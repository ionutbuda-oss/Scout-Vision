## Distinctions

- **Distribution** describes safe and purposeful circulation of possession.
- **Progression** describes advancement of possession toward the opposition goal.
- **Ball Carrying** describes advancement through dribbling and offensive duels.
- **Creativity** describes penetrative passing that disrupts defensive structure.
- **Chance Creation** describes the production of scoring opportunities.
- **Combination Play** describes a winger's participation in attacking combinations.
- **Link Play** describes a striker's ability to connect attacks with teammates.
- **Offensive Duels** describes a striker's ground-duel performance against defenders.
- **Ball Security** remains explicitly documented as a statistical proxy.

No KPI, KPI weight, competency weight or score formula was changed.
