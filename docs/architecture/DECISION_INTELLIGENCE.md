# ScoutVision Decision Theory

Decision Intelligence is the reasoning layer of ScoutVision.

Its purpose is not to classify players.

Its purpose is to transform objective football evidence into explainable,
reproducible and trustworthy scouting decisions.

Every decision produced by ScoutVision must answer five questions:

1. What is the player's identity?

2. Why was this identity selected?

3. How confident is this decision?

4. How stable is this decision?

5. What should the scout do next?

---

# ScoutVision Decision Principles

Decision Intelligence is built on five engineering principles.

## Principle 1 — Evidence First

Every conclusion must originate from objective football evidence.

No subjective assumptions are allowed.

---

## Principle 2 — Explainability

Every decision must be explainable.

If ScoutVision cannot explain a conclusion, the conclusion is incomplete.

---

## Principle 3 — Determinism

The same player evaluated with the same data must always produce the same decision.

---

## Principle 4 — Uncertainty

Every decision has a measurable level of uncertainty.

Confidence, Stability and Decision Quality must always accompany the final decision.

---

## Principle 5 — Traceability

Every conclusion must be traceable back to the evidence that generated it.

# Decision Philosophy

ScoutVision does not make decisions from raw data.

ScoutVision first transforms data into evidence.

Evidence is analysed.

Reasoning is applied.

Only then is a scouting decision produced.

Every recommendation must be supported by transparent evidence and a complete decision trace.


---

# Identity Confidence Model

Identity Confidence is not a single metric.

It is a composite intelligence model built from multiple independent
components.

Each component evaluates one aspect of the decision.

The final confidence score is obtained by combining all components.

Identity Confidence

=

Gap Component

+

Dominance Component

+

Distribution Component

+

Consistency Component

+

Future Components

The architecture is intentionally extensible.

New confidence components can be introduced without modifying the
existing model.
