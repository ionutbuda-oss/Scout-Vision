# ScoutVision Position Philosophy v2.0

---

# CENTRE BACK (CB)

## Primary mission

Protect the goal by preventing opponents from progressing and initiating possession from the defensive line.

---

## Core Competencies (mandatory)

DEFENDING

A centre back must consistently win defensive actions and protect the penalty area.

Without strong defending, no other competency can compensate.

---

## Primary Competencies

DISTRIBUTION

Ability to safely circulate possession and break the first pressing line.

PROGRESSION

Ability to advance possession through progressive passing.

---

## Supporting Competencies

BALL CARRYING

Useful for breaking pressure but not essential.

---

## Not considered

GOAL THREAT

CHANCE CREATION

OFFENSIVE DUELS

These competencies should not materially influence centre back recruitment.

---

## Recruitment Philosophy

A centre back is recruited primarily for:

1. Defensive reliability
2. Ball circulation
3. Progressing possession

Everything else is secondary.
